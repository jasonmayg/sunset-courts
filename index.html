Game to

Play as the Sunsets: you control whoever has the ball, and on defense the player closest to it. WASD to move, Shift to sprint, hold Space and let go in the gold band to shoot, E to pass or steal, Q to switch defenders. Sprint at the rim to dunk. On a half court, clear the ball past the arc after every change of possession.
